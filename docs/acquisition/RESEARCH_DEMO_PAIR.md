## Why it is the representative demo pair

- Both records use the exact same Work24 occupation and the same open-ended
  employment category.
- Both state pay, working hours, concrete food R&D duties, and entry
  conditions.
- The Jeonbuk posting has a higher stated salary floor, which makes the demo
  useful for correcting an information gap without claiming that either
  employer is better.
- Neither posting states complete probation pay/period or a structured
  onboarding system. Those omissions remain visible and become verification
  questions; they were not filled with assumptions.
